Hackathon Project

Proposal Study Hub – A Localized Learning Management System Project Context This project is developed by the students of Shree Janta Secondary School for the hackathon competition hosted by SOS School.

Project Overview Study Hub is a learning platform designed for students and learners who struggle to find free, reliable, and easily accessible study materials, notes, and educational PDF books online. Our goal is to provide a centralized platform where learners can conveniently access the resources they need for their studies. Our Vision Study Hub is a localized Learning Management System (LMS) designed to help bridge the digital education gap in Nepal. Many schools, colleges, and educational institutions rely on scattered resources and platforms that can be difficult to access, particularly for students with limited internet connectivity. Our platform aims to bring learning resources together in one place, offering downloadable notes and study materials tailored to Nepal's education system, including SEE and +2 levels. By making educational resources free and accessible, we hope to make learning free, convenient, and engaging for students across Nepal.

Technology Used We primarily used HTML and CSS to develop our website's structure and design.In the future, we plan to expand its functionality by integrating JavaScript and Python, enabling more interactive features and improved capabilities.
